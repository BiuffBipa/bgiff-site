The Gateway

Where Independent Cinema Passes Through

Berlin has always been a city of crossings — of walls that fell, of borders opened, of voices set free. Berlin Gate International Film Festival carries that spirit into cinema.

We are an independent, international festival built to discover new voices and connect them with audiences and industry. Our mission stands on three pillars: a launchpad of prestige and discovery for filmmakers, a global hybrid platform reaching audiences everywhere, and a curated, memorable festival experience in the heart of Berlin.

Every format is welcome — from a first student short to a feature by a seasoned auteur. What unites the selection is originality, courage, and craft.

Jury & Judging

Official Selection

Every entry is reviewed by our programming committee. Selected works receive the official laurel and enter competition.

Professional Jury

An independent jury of working film professionals decides the winners, judging artistic quality, storytelling, craft and impact. Jury announced ahead of the season.

Audience Award

Audiences vote online for their favourite — a prize decided by the people, celebrating films that move a crowd.
